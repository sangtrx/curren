### Short description — ~245 characters

Curren turns each market decision into a durable record with provenance, lifecycle updates and a terminal outcome. Humans use Curren Pro; trading teams and software agents consume the same bounded state through a read-only API and MCP interface.

### Problem — compact

Markets have abundant data, alerts and AI commentary, but a decision often loses the evidence trail that matters: what was actually recommended before the move, what context existed then, what changed later and what the final outcome was. Humans reconstruct that manually; software agents need explicit provenance, timestamps, state and outcomes.

### Solution — compact

Curren makes each market decision a durable, machine-readable lifecycle record: original thesis/setup, timestamp and provenance, bounded context, entry/invalidation/target/expiry rules, append-only updates, terminal outcome and verification. Humans consume it through Curren Pro; teams and agents use the same bounded state through a read-only API/MCP surface.

### Why now — compact

AI agents are moving from reading financial information to continuously consuming structured context. Automated workflows cannot rely on screenshots or mutable narrative feeds; they need explicit state, provenance, replay safety and outcomes. Crypto is the fastest beachhead because it is 24/7, programmable and already combines humans, bots, APIs and agent workflows.

### Initial customer — compact

Small crypto trading teams and builders of trading/financial agents that already operate automated or semi-automated workflows. They often monitor multiple feeds, parse messages, reconstruct lifecycle state and evaluate sources manually. Serious active traders are a secondary validation/distribution market through Curren Pro.

### Business model — compact

Curren Intelligence is one product with two initial commercial surfaces: Curren Pro for humans and Curren API for machine/team access. Curren is pre-revenue; pricing and packaging remain hypotheses until willingness to pay is observed.
